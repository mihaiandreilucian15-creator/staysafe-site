How it works

StaySafe is designed to be simple, fast and useful exactly when you do not have time to search through your phone.

🚨

Trigger

You press the SOS button or the app detects a possible fall and starts the emergency flow.

📍

Alert

Your emergency contact receives an SOS message with your GPS location.

❤️

Get help

Emergency information and fast alerts can help family or responders act sooner.
