Why StaySafe

The problem is not simply the absence of another SOS app. The real problem is the time lost between an emergency event and the moment help is mobilized.

⏱️

Reaction speed

StaySafe is designed to reduce the time between an emergency, family notification and emergency response.

📍

Immediate context

The app can share location, display medical information and trigger different workflows for Medical and Danger situations.

🌍

Scalability

The model can expand toward families, seniors, companies, universities, wearables and safety services.
